🧮 Can You Keep the Club Alive and in the Green?

Welcome to Club Budget: The Game!, an upbeat financial simulation and math arcade game where you take the driver's seat as the newly appointed treasurer of the school's math club!

Your mission sounds simple: navigate an eventful school season, keep the club solvent, and balance the competing demands of eager students, an ambitious coach, and detail-oriented parents. But between unexpected bus repairs, customized team hoodies, pizza parties, and surprise grants, every dollar counts—and one bad calculation could spark a club rebellion or financial bankruptcy!

✨ Key Features

  • ⚖️ Tri-Stakeholder Morale Balancing: It’s not just about the money! Keep three passionate club leaders happy throughout the season:
    • Piper (Student Rep): Wants fun activities, cool gear, and memorable events.
    • Coach John (Head Coach): Prioritizes competition readiness, training supplies, and team performance.
    • Ms. Chaidee (Parent Booster): Demands responsible budgeting, safety, and transparent accounting.
    Watch out! If any leader's morale drops below 40%, you risk a student mutiny, coach resignation, or parent boycott!
  • 🧠 28+ Handcrafted Scenarios & Surprise Events: No two seasons are the same. Face dynamic dilemmas drawn from non-repeating decks covering uniforms, tournament travel, guest speakers, fundraisers, and surprise equipment malfunctions.
  • ⚡ Real-World Math Challenges: Put 5th Grade Common Core & Middle School math skills to work! Calculate bulk discounts, unit prices, sales tax, ticket revenue splits, and dimension-based material costs. Solve challenges correctly to unlock sponsor discounts, score prize bonuses, and preserve club cash!
  • ⏱️ Optional Pressure-Cooker Timers: Play at your own pace in Untimed Mode, or kick the adrenaline up a notch with 30s, 60s, 90s, or 120s decision countdowns!
  • 🛠️ Full Game Customization: Tailor your challenge with adjustable sliders:
    • Club Roster: 4 to 36 students
    • Starting Budget: $100 to $1,000
    • Season Length: 4 to 28 weeks
  • 🎉 Arcade Juice & Polish: Enjoy satisfying rolling budget counters, floating transaction alerts, bouncy character emotions, confetti particle cannons on big wins, and screen shakes when stakes run high!
  • 🎵 Catchy Soundtrack & Polyphonic SFX: Upbeat, cozy chiptune-inspired tunes and crisp arcade sound effects with instant mute toggle (M key).

🎮 Controls & How to Play

Control Action
Mouse Click Select options, submit math answers, adjust setup sliders.
Keys 1 / 2 / 3 Quick-select weekly club options.
Space / Enter Confirm modals and continue to next week.
M Key Toggle music and sound effects mute.
F Key Toggle Fullscreen mode.
Esc Key Open Exit Game confirmation menu.

🎓 Perfect for the Classroom & Home

Designed for upper elementary and middle school students (Grades 5+), Club Budget: The Game! teaches essential financial literacy and practical mathematical reasoning in an intuitive, engaging format:

  • Multi-digit multiplication and division in context.
  • Fractions, percentages, and unit rates applied to real purchases.
  • Budget forecasting, resource tradeoffs, and reserve planning.
  • Critical thinking under constraints and stakeholder negotiations.
